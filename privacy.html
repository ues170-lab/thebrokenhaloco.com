Short version: We collect your email when you sign up for launch updates or the free worksheet. We use it to send book/drop news and tools. We do not sell your data. You can unsubscribe anytime.

When you submit the launch list form on thebrokenhaloco.com, we may collect:

Your information is used to:

Form submissions are processed by Formspree on behalf of Broken Halo Co. Formspree stores submission data so we can manage the launch list and reply when needed.
