THE BOOK

A raw memoir about the identity addiction builds.

Broken But Breathing is not a clean redemption arc. It is a direct look at addiction, shame, relapse, incarceration, leadership, public failure, and the long fight to stop confusing survival patterns with identity.

The cover hits the same visual world as the brand: cracked halo, glass, cyan/pink damage, and the book title carrying weight before anyone reads page one.

THE STORY BEHIND IT

What if trauma glowed?

Broken Halo Co. exists for people who survived things they rarely talk about. The damage is not hidden here. The damage becomes part of the design.

The book and the brand come from the same place: addiction, identity, shame, relapse, rebuilding, and the uncomfortable truth that sometimes the life that saves you feels unfamiliar at first.

This is for the ones who are still breathing, still building, and done pretending the scars did not teach them anything.

Eric Lee Sanders, SUDCC II
Founder, Broken Halo Co. · Author, Broken But Breathing · Kern County, California
