Guilt + Shame
Field Worksheet

This is for the part of recovery nobody wants to make cute: the difference between owning what happened and turning yourself into the sentence.

Core truth

Guilt says, “I did something that needs repair.” Shame says, “I am the damage.”

The work is not pretending nothing happened. The work is telling the truth without letting the worst chapter become your whole identity.

1. Name the guilt without dressing it up

2. Separate repair from punishment

3. The companion thought

Write the thought that keeps following you around. Then answer it like someone trying to rebuild instead of self-destruct.

4. Evidence this week

Do not argue with shame all day. Build evidence against it.

Closing line

You can be responsible without being reduced to the wreckage.
